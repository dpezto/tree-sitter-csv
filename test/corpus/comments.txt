================================
Dates embedded in prose
:language(csv)
:language(psv)
:language(tsv)
================================
# Created 2026-11-01; due 02/11/2026.
# At 12:34 PM and 2026-11-02T01:02:03.456-06:00
# End 2026-11-03
--------------------------------
(document (comment (date) (date)) (comment (date) (date)) (comment (date)))
